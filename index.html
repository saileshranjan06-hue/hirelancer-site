The Problem

Good marketing talent is expensive. Bad marketing is worse.

A performance marketer with 4–6 years of real, campaign-tested experience commands a full-time salary most small and mid-size businesses can't stretch to. So businesses settle: a fresher who's still learning, a generalist agency juggling twenty clients, or a family member who "knows Instagram." Budgets get spent, results don't show up, and the business concludes marketing doesn't work for them — when really, the wrong person was running it.

Meanwhile, hundreds of experienced performance marketers actively choose to freelance — for flexibility, for variety, for control over their client list. That talent already exists. It's just never been easy for a business to find, vet, and hire it safely. That's the specific gap Hirelancer.agency closes.

Is This For You?

Built for businesses stuck between "too expensive" and "not good enough"

Hirelancer is a good fit if you...

  • Run a business in real estate, insurance, healthcare, e-commerce or a similar sector and need consistent lead flow

Common Questions

Before you reach out

What's the minimum monthly budget to use Hirelancer?

There's no fixed minimum — it depends on your sector and the scope of work. Most businesses we work with budget somewhere between ₹25,000–₹1,00,000/month for their freelancer. Tell us your number and we'll be upfront if it's realistic for the sector you're in.

What if I don't like anyone in the shortlist?

We keep sourcing until you find the right fit — there's no charge for interviewing candidates, and no obligation to hire from the first shortlist we send.

Do you manage the marketer's work after I hire them?

No. Once you hire, you manage the working relationship directly — scope, deliverables, communication. We're the matchmaker, not a middle-man agency sitting between you and your marketer.

How is this different from a marketplace like Upwork or Fiverr?

On open marketplaces, you sift through hundreds of unverified profiles yourself. We pre-screen for real performance marketing experience, match to your specific sector and budget, and only send you candidates worth interviewing.

Can I hire more than one marketer through Hirelancer?

Yes — many businesses start with one freelancer and later add specialists (e.g. a separate marketer for a new channel or region). Each hire is a separate one-time 10% fee.
