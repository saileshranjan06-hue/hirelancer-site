How to Hire a Freelance Performance Marketer for Your D2C Brand

By Hirelancer.agency Team · Published August 17, 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answer: Hire a freelance performance marketer who can show real ROAS numbers from past Meta and Google Shopping campaigns, not just reach or impressions. Look for hands-on experience with catalog feeds, creative testing, and retargeting. Expect to pay roughly ₹30,000 to ₹1,20,000+ per month, depending on experience and how much ad spend they'll manage.

D2C brands run on a different rhythm than most businesses.

Sales happen directly through paid ads, not through a sales team or a showroom. The marketer you hire is effectively driving revenue in real time.

A generalist who has only run brand-awareness campaigns usually is not the right fit here. This guide covers what to check before you hire, and what a fair monthly rate looks like.

Why D2C performance marketing is a specialized skill

Most D2C revenue comes from a small number of paid channels, mainly Meta ads and Google Shopping, all working off the same product catalog feed.

A marketer who understands this world is comfortable with:

  • Catalog feed hygiene, making sure product titles, images, prices, and availability sync correctly
  • Dynamic retargeting for cart abandoners and past visitors
  • Fast creative testing, sometimes five or more ad variations a week to stay ahead of ad fatigue

If a candidate's past work is mostly lead-generation campaigns for local businesses, that experience doesn't transfer cleanly to a store selling products at checkout.

What a good D2C performance marketer does day to day

The work centers on a few repeating cycles:

  • Reviewing blended ROAS and new-customer acquisition cost across channels
  • Pausing or scaling ad sets based on that data
  • Briefing new creative to keep up with ad fatigue
  • Troubleshooting catalog feed errors that can quietly tank Shopping ad performance

A strong candidate should be able to walk you through a recent week of this work in specific terms, not just describe it in general language.

What to check before you hire

Before you commit, run through this short checklist:

  • Ask for a screen-share walkthrough of a real Meta Ads Manager or Google Ads account they've managed (anonymized if needed)
  • Ask specifically about their new customer acquisition cost, not just blended ROAS, since blended numbers can look strong while new customer growth quietly stalls
  • Ask how many creative variations they typically test per month, and how they decide what to kill versus scale
  • Ask whether they've handled a full catalog feed setup themselves, since feed problems are one of the most common silent performance killers in e-commerce ads

Common mistakes D2C founders make when hiring

  • Trusting screenshots without context. A ₹5,000 spend showing 8x ROAS over one week means very little at scale.
  • Hiring a "social media marketer" instead of a performance marketer. Good creative design isn't the same as managing a paid ad budget with a P&L attached to it.
  • Skipping reference checks. A short call with a past client usually surfaces whether someone actually delivered results or just managed the account. Our guide to vetting a freelance performance marketer has a full checklist for this.

What a realistic budget looks like

For a D2C brand doing meaningful volume, freelance performance marketers typically charge ₹30,000 to ₹1,20,000+ per month for their management fee, separate from ad spend itself. This is in line with the broader rate ranges in our freelance performance marketer cost guide.

Someone managing a smaller catalog with modest ad spend and one or two channels typically sits toward the lower end. Someone managing multi-channel campaigns with higher ad spend and a track record of scaling brands sits toward the higher end.

Signs a candidate isn't ready for your ad budget

A few patterns are worth asking follow-up questions about, even if they're not automatic dealbreakers:

  • They can't explain why a specific ad set was paused or scaled, beyond "the algorithm decided". That usually means they're reading dashboards rather than actively managing strategy.
  • Their only past experience is with brands spending under ₹20,000 a month on ads. Managing ₹2,00,000+ a month takes genuinely different skills, like bid strategy, budget pacing, and avoiding audience overlap, so ask directly how they'd adjust at your scale.
  • They're vague about which parts of a past campaign were their own decision versus a senior colleague's, if they worked at an agency. Agency account managers sometimes execute a strategy someone else designed rather than building one themselves.

Frequently asked questions

What should I pay a freelance performance marketer for a D2C brand?

Most D2C-focused freelance performance marketers charge between ₹30,000 and ₹1,20,000+ per month, depending on experience, the number of channels they manage, and your monthly ad spend. This is separate from the ad spend itself.

Is ROAS the only metric I should ask about?

No. Blended ROAS matters, but also ask about new customer acquisition cost, creative testing cadence, and catalog feed health, since a marketer who only optimizes for ROAS on existing customers can make your numbers look good while new customer growth stalls.

Can one freelancer handle both Meta and Google Shopping ads for my store?

Yes, many experienced D2C performance marketers manage both channels together, since they often share the same product catalog feed and retargeting audiences. Ask for specific examples of stores where they ran both simultaneously.
