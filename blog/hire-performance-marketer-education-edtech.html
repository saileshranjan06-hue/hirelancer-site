How Ed-Tech and Coaching Institutes Can Hire the Right Performance Marketer

By Hirelancer.agency Team · Published August 21, 2026 · 7 min read
Quick answer: Hire a freelance performance marketer with real admissions-marketing experience, not a generalist. Look for someone who tracks cost per application, understands the admission-cycle calendar, and can run separate messaging for parents and students. Expect to pay roughly ₹25,000 to ₹1,20,000+ per month depending on experience.

Admissions marketing runs on a different clock than most other sectors.

Demand spikes around specific admission windows, then drops off. A marketer who's only run always-on e-commerce or local-business campaigns often doesn't plan for this rhythm.

This guide covers what's actually different about hiring for ed-tech and coaching institutes, and what to check before you bring someone on.

Why admissions marketing needs sector-specific experience

Three things make this sector distinct from most others we cover:

  • Seasonality. Spend needs to ramp up ahead of admission cycles and pace itself through the window, not run flat all year.
  • Cost per application, not just cost per lead. A cheap lead that never fills out an application form isn't worth much.
  • Two audiences, one campaign. Parents and students often respond to different messaging, sometimes within the same household.

A marketer without this specific background will usually run a single generic campaign and miss all three.

What a good ed-tech marketer actually does

Day to day, the work centers on a few things:

  • Planning ad spend around the admission-cycle calendar, ramping up before key deadlines
  • Tracking cost per application, and separately, application-to-enrollment rate
  • Testing separate creative and messaging angles for parents versus students
  • Coordinating lead handoff speed with the counseling or admissions team, since a slow handoff loses interested applicants

For a broader sense of what the role covers generally, see our guide to what a performance marketer actually does.

What to check before you hire

Ask specifically about admissions experience, not general lead-gen work.

  • Ask for a past example of how they paced spend across an admission cycle, not just a single always-on campaign.
  • Ask how they separated messaging for parents versus students in a past campaign, and what changed between the two.
  • Ask how quickly leads were routed to counselors, and whether they tracked lead-to-enrollment, not just lead volume.
  • Ask whether they've worked with schools, coaching institutes, or ed-tech platforms specifically, since the buying psychology differs from most other sectors.

A common mistake: optimizing for leads instead of enrollments

It's easy for a campaign to look successful on paper while quietly underperforming where it matters.

A marketer chasing a low cost-per-lead target can flood you with form fills from people who were never seriously considering enrolling. The number that actually matters is cost per enrolled student, or at minimum cost per completed application.

Ask any candidate directly which of these two numbers they optimized toward in past roles, and expect a clear answer, not a vague one.

What a realistic budget looks like

Freelance performance marketers with ed-tech or admissions experience typically charge ₹25,000 to ₹1,20,000+ per month for their management fee, separate from ad spend. This is in line with the broader ranges in our freelance performance marketer cost guide.

Institutions running a single admission cycle with a modest budget typically sit toward the lower end. Larger institutes running multi-channel campaigns across several intake cycles a year sit toward the higher end.

Frequently asked questions

What makes performance marketing for ed-tech and coaching institutes different?

Admissions run on a seasonal cycle rather than year-round demand, campaigns need to track cost per application rather than just leads, and messaging usually has to work for two different audiences: parents and students, who respond to different appeals.

What should I pay a freelance performance marketer for an ed-tech or coaching business?

Rates typically range from ₹25,000 to ₹1,20,000+ per month depending on experience and the number of channels managed, separate from ad spend, in line with broader freelance performance marketer rates.

How do I know if counselor lead handoff is being handled well?

Ask the marketer how quickly leads are routed to counselors, whether they track lead-to-enrollment conversion rather than just lead volume, and how they've coordinated with a counseling or admissions team in past campaigns.
