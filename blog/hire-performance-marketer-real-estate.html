How to Hire a Freelance Performance Marketer for Your Real Estate Business

By Hirelancer.agency Team · Published August 8, 2026 · 8 min read
Quick answer: Look for a freelancer with a verifiable track record running Meta and Google ad campaigns specifically for property listings, not just general small-business ads. Budget ₹30,000–₹90,000/month depending on scope, ask to see real (anonymized) campaign results before hiring, and start with a defined 90-day test period rather than an open-ended contract.

Real estate lead generation runs on a small set of channels — Meta ads, Google Search and Display, WhatsApp follow-up, and increasingly property portal advertising. A performance marketer who's actually run campaigns in this space understands things a generalist won't: how to structure ad sets around specific projects or listings, how to filter out tire-kickers from serious buyers, and how to keep cost-per-lead sane in a category where clicks are expensive.

What to look for in a candidate

Beyond general performance marketing skill, a real estate-specific freelancer should be able to speak concretely about:

  • Listing-level campaign structure — running ads at the project or property level rather than one generic "contact us" campaign for the whole brokerage.
  • Lead qualification — using forms, WhatsApp bots, or call tracking to separate genuinely interested buyers from people just browsing.
  • Retargeting site visitors and no-shows — real estate has a long consideration cycle, so a good marketer will have a retargeting sequence for people who didn't convert on the first visit.
  • Local market knowledge — even if not hyper-local, they should understand how location-based targeting affects cost and lead quality in real estate specifically.

Questions worth asking in the interview

  1. "Walk me through a real estate campaign you've run — what was the cost per lead, and how did you improve it over time?"
  2. "How do you handle lead quality versus lead volume when a client wants both?"
  3. "What's your process for a new listing launch versus an ongoing evergreen campaign?"
  4. "How do you report results, and how often?"

Vague answers to the first question — no real numbers, no mention of iteration — are a warning sign. Real estate performance marketing is measurable, and someone with genuine experience will have specifics.

Typical cost

Freelance performance marketers serving real estate businesses typically charge ₹30,000–₹90,000 per month as a management fee, separate from ad spend. Rate depends on ad budget managed, number of active listings or projects, and how many channels are in play (just Meta, or Meta plus Google plus WhatsApp automation). This is a fraction of what a full-time in-house senior marketer would cost in salary and benefits.

A simple way to reduce hiring risk

Rather than committing to a long-term contract immediately, agree on a defined first period — 60 to 90 days — with clear, specific goals (e.g., a target cost-per-qualified-lead). This gives both sides a low-risk way to confirm fit before scaling the relationship.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a freelance real estate performance marketer cost?

Most experienced freelance performance marketers working with real estate brokerages and developers charge between ₹30,000 and ₹90,000 per month depending on ad spend managed, number of channels, and experience level.

What platforms should a real estate performance marketer be experienced with?

Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads are the core two, since most real estate lead generation runs through them. WhatsApp-based lead qualification and property portal advertising are useful additional skills.

Should I hire a freelancer or a real estate marketing agency?

A freelancer typically costs less and gives you a direct working relationship with the person running your campaigns, which suits most single-brokerage or small-developer budgets. Agencies make more sense for very large, multi-market operations needing a full team.
