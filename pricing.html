Pricing

One fee. Paid once. Nothing else.

We only get paid when you successfully hire. No subscription, no retainer, no ongoing commission on your freelancer's pay.

Our fee

10%

of the freelancer's agreed monthly rate — charged once, when you hire.

How the fee works

  • Charged only once, at the point of hire — not every month
  • Calculated as 10% of the monthly rate you and the freelancer agree on
  • No fee at all if you don't end up hiring anyone
  • No markup added to the freelancer's pay — you and they set the rate directly
  • No long-term contracts or lock-in with Hirelancer.agency

Pricing Questions

Common questions about the fee

When exactly do I pay the 10% fee?

After you've interviewed candidates and confirmed a hire — not before, and not if you decide not to proceed with anyone.

What if the freelancer and I later change the agreed rate?

The fee is calculated on the rate agreed at the time of hire. If your engagement scope changes later and the rate is renegotiated, that's between you and the freelancer directly.

Is there a refund if the freelancer doesn't work out?

Reach out to us if a placement isn't working — we'll discuss options on a case-by-case basis, which may include resourcing a replacement at no additional fee.

Do you take a cut of the freelancer's monthly pay going forward?

No. The 10% is a one-time matching fee. Every rupee you pay the freelancer afterward goes to them, in full.
